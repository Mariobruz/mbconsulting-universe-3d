SchermiAIProduct catalogue software, automatic website and AI voice assistant in one platform

Catalogue, website and voice. Together.

What is SchermiAI

One platform connects three things: the company catalogue, the public website and an AI assistant that talks to customers.

The browser-based management system includes over 600 product records across 24 categories, photos and technical details, 18 downloadable PDF price lists, customer records and orders with automatically calculated discounts and totals. Access is password-protected.

The same catalogue powers the public website, with 29 pages, search and filters. Management data updates the website without manually rewriting pages.

Simona is available on every page. Visitors can speak to the AI assistant to explain their needs, explore product categories and leave their contact details, delivered by email. Everything runs on cloud infrastructure.

Key features

  • Over 600 product records in 24 categories with photos and specs
  • 18 downloadable PDF price lists
  • Customer and order records with automatic discounts and totals
  • Public website generated from the catalogue, with search and filters
  • Simona, the AI voice assistant that guides customers and collects leads
  • Protected access and cloud infrastructure

Who it is for

For companies with large catalogues that want management software, website and customer service always in sync.
