The MaskOnline ticketing for theatres and events: reserved seats, payment and QR-code tickets

Your next show starts here.

What is The Mask

A complete reserved-seat ticketing system. Audiences choose their seats on an interactive seating plan, pay by card or PayPal and receive a QR-code ticket by email. Staff scan tickets with a phone at the entrance, while management tracks admissions in real time.

Key features

  • Seat selection on the interactive seating plan
  • Payment by card or PayPal
  • QR-code ticket sent by email
  • Entrance check with staff smartphones
  • Real-time admission tracking for management

Who it is for

For theatres, cinemas, auditoriums, show organisers and cultural associations.
