Your entire fleet. One place.

What is Rentora

A web platform that lets car rental agencies manage everything from one dashboard: vehicle fleets, calendar bookings, customer records and rental agreements ready to print. Customers have a private area to book online and find their contracts and rental history.

Each agency works with its own separate data. Registration includes a seven-day free trial, while a central administration panel oversees all active agencies.

Key features

  • Vehicle fleet, bookings and calendar in one dashboard
  • Customer records and print-ready rental agreements
  • Customer area for online booking and contract history
  • Multi-agency system with separate data
  • Central administration panel
  • 7-day free trial

Who it is for

For car rental, rent-a-car and van rental agencies that want to manage everything online.
