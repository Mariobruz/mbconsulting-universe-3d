SimonaAI voice assistant that answers customers instead of voicemail

Every conversation, a possibility.

What is Simona

An AI voice assistant that talks to your customers instead of sending them to voicemail. It answers immediately, understands what they need, collects their name and email address, and emails you a summary. No missed calls or messages to replay: you receive a contact ready for a callback.

Key features

  • Answers immediately, at any hour
  • Understands what the customer needs
  • Collects name and email
  • Emails you a summary of the conversation
  • No missed calls, no messages to replay

Who it is for

For professionals, shops and small businesses that cannot always pick up and do not want to lose customers.
