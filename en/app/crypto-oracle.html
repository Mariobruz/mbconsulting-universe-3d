What is Crypto Oracle v2

A six-agent system that monitors the crypto market around the clock and sends Telegram alerts only when something deserves attention. Agents examine prices and volumes, derivatives, blockchain data, international news and social conversations. An alert is sent only when at least two independent sources agree.

Decisions remain with the user: the system provides suggestions and does not buy or sell automatically.

Key features

  • Six AI agents watching the market around the clock
  • Prices, volumes and derivatives
  • On-chain blockchain data
  • International news and social sentiment
  • Alerts only when at least two independent sources agree
  • No automatic trading: the system suggests, you decide

Who it is for

For crypto followers who want a few reliable alerts instead of dozens of notifications a day.
