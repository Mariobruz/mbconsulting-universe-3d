Independent stories. Boundless vision.

What is Ciakstream

A streaming platform dedicated to Italian and international independent cinema: films, documentaries, shorts, debut works and vertical movies.

Viewers can browse by category, resume playback, read reviews and join a community with posts, comments, a forum, a blog and a festival with audience voting.

Filmmakers have their own area to upload work for free, track statistics and create private invitation-only screening rooms. The platform curates works rather than selling upload space.

Key features

  • Catalogue of films, documentaries and shorts browsable by category
  • Player that resumes where you left off
  • Reviews, community posts and comments, forum and blog
  • Online festival with audience voting
  • Filmmaker area: free uploads and viewing statistics
  • Private invitation-only screening rooms

Who it is for

For independent cinema lovers and for directors, filmmakers and production companies looking for a curated showcase with no upload fees.
