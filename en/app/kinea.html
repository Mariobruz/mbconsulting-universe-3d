KineaGym and fitness centre management software: class booking, memberships and members

More energy. Less admin.

What is Kinea

A web app covering the full life of a gym, from individual class bookings to membership renewals.

Members sign in with a six-digit PIN, view the weekly calendar and book independently. They can change sessions, join waiting lists, make up classes cancelled in time and buy extra sessions.

Managers have a daily agenda, member records, expiring memberships, notifications, blocked dates and times, session capacity controls and Excel reports.

Automatic features include membership and medical certificate expiry reminders, a weekly programme sent every Sunday and data backups.

Key features

  • Members book classes from the weekly calendar with a PIN
  • Session changes, waiting lists, make-up classes and extra sessions
  • Daily agenda, member records and expiring memberships
  • Capacity per time slot and blocked days and hours
  • Automatic membership and medical certificate reminders
  • Weekly schedule every Sunday, Excel reports and backups

Who it is for

For gyms, pilates and yoga studios, boxes and fitness centres that want less admin and happier members.
