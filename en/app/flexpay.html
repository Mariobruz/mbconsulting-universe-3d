FlexPayOnline payment system for websites and online stores: cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay

Payments made simple.

What is FlexPay

FlexPay is a payment system that integrates into a website or online store with a few lines of code. Clicking “Pay” opens a secure checkout for credit cards, Apple Pay or Google Pay. After payment, the customer returns automatically to your website.

There is nothing to install or manage. Payment networks handle 3D Secure and card data protection, and funds go directly to the merchant’s account.

Key features

  • Integrates into any website or online store with a few lines of code
  • Secure checkout with credit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Automatic return to your site after payment
  • 3D Secure banking security handled by the card networks
  • Funds paid directly into the merchant's account
  • Nothing to install or manage

Who it is for

For online shops, professionals and businesses that want to take payments on their own website simply and securely.
