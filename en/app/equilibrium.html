What is Equilibrium

A personal area where patients can find their diet, appointments and progress in one place.

Book an available appointment at your practice, consult your meal-by-meal nutrition plan and track weight changes over time with a chart.

Access is personal and protected: data is visible only to the patient and their doctor.

Key features

  • Meal-by-meal nutrition plan
  • Appointment booking in the practice's available slots
  • Weight progress chart over time
  • Appointments and progress always at hand
  • Personal, protected access: data visible only to patient and doctor

Who it is for

For nutritionists, dietitians and medical practices that want to offer patients a modern, secure personal area.
