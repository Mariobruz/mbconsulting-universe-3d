What is Scanner ISBN

Scanner ISBN turns any book collection into an organised digital library, accessible from a smartphone, tablet or computer.

Scan an ISBN barcode or photograph a cover. AI recognises the book and retrieves its title, author, publisher and bibliographic details.

Organise books into custom shelves, manage reading and loans, explore statistics, estimate second-hand values and export your collection to Excel or a PDF catalogue.

Key features

  • Scan the ISBN barcode or photograph the cover
  • AI recognition of title, author, publisher and bibliographic data
  • Custom shelves, reading and lending
  • Statistics and used book value estimates
  • Export to Excel or a PDF catalogue
  • Works on smartphone, tablet and computer

Who it is for

For readers, collectors, bookshops, school libraries and associations that want an organised catalogue.
