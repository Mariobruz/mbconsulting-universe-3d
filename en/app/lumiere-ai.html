What is Lumiere AI

Lumiere AI is an online platform for creating videos, images and audio from a simple written description, without editing software or technical expertise.

It brings together different AI models in one interface, alongside a chat assistant that helps you write prompts and improve the results.

It runs directly in your browser, supports four languages and uses a credit system with flexible subscription plans, from an initial trial to intensive professional use.

Key features

  • Videos, images and audio from a simple written description
  • Multiple AI models in one interface
  • Chat assistant that helps write prompts and improve results
  • No editing software or technical skills needed
  • Runs in the browser, available in four languages
  • Credit system with flexible subscription plans

Who it is for

For creators, social media managers, agencies and businesses that want to produce visual content in minutes.
