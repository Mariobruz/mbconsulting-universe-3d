What is Mappix

A living map of Calabria: nearby places, routes and local businesses. Proximity-based offers, navigation and content in five languages. A model that can be replicated across any Italian region.

Key features

  • Places, routes and businesses near your location
  • Proximity offers from local businesses
  • Built-in navigation to every point
  • Content in five languages for international visitors
  • A model that can be replicated in any Italian region

Who it is for

For tourists and residents, municipalities, tourism boards and local businesses that want to be found.
