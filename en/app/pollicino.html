PollicinoA safe phone for children: call family with a single touch

One touch to feel closer.

What is Pollicino

A phone designed around one gesture: touch a photo of Mum, Dad, a grandparent or another relative to call. No numbers to dial, address book to navigate or apps to open. The screen shows only family faces and stays locked to that view, without games, ads or calls from strangers.

Calls use a private line configured for your family. Only authorised numbers can connect. It works over Wi-Fi or mobile data, including across countries, and calls between family phones are free.

• One touch, one person: a large button with each relative’s name and photo.

• No distractions: the phone stays on its dedicated screen.

• Family-only calls: unknown numbers cannot get through.

• Easy to answer: just touch the screen.

• Ready to use: phones arrive configured with your contacts.

Key features

  • One large button with name and photo for each relative
  • Locked to a single screen: no games, ads or apps
  • Calls only with authorised numbers on a private line
  • Works on wi-fi or mobile data, even across countries
  • Free calls between family phones
  • Arrives pre-configured, name by name

Who it is for

For parents who want a safe first phone for their child, and for grandparents and relatives far away.
