What is Spesa Smart

Say your shopping list aloud and Simona tells you where it costs less. Spesa Smart compares supermarket flyer prices near you and calculates savings for each supermarket, identifying the lowest price. No registration or password: share your location and speak. Simona recognises returning visitors and remembers their habits.

• Speak instead of typing: Simona listens to your list and reads prices aloud.

• Prices from nearby supermarket flyers, with distances and calculated savings.

• No account, email or password required.

Key features

  • Spoken shopping list, prices read aloud
  • Real prices from nearby chain flyers
  • Distance in km and savings for each supermarket
  • Lowest price shown product by product
  • No registration: just share your location
  • Simona recognises you and remembers your habits

Who it is for

For families and anyone who wants to save on groceries without browsing dozens of flyers.
