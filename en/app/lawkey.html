Legal knowledge. Always with you.

What is LawKey

LawKey is the legal assistant you can carry in your pocket. Launch it from a USB drive with a double click on Mac or Windows. It works completely offline: no law firm data leaves your computer, with no server or cloud required.

The drive contains indexed Italian legislation and case law, with over 190,000 searchable text passages. Ask a question in natural language and LawKey returns relevant provisions and judgments in under a second, with precise citations ready to copy into your document.

Key features

  • Launches from a USB drive with a double click on Mac and Windows
  • Works completely offline: no data leaves your computer
  • Italian codes and case law already indexed: over 190,000 passages
  • Natural-language questions, answers in under a second
  • Articles and rulings with exact citations ready to paste

Who it is for

For lawyers, trainees and law firms that want AI legal research without sending confidential data to servers or the cloud.
